## 1. Research question and hypothesis

Can DQN with experience replay and a target network learn to stay on the track in CarRacing when the environment uses discrete actions?

**Hypothesis:** The agent will achieve positive evaluation reward after approximately 300,000 environment steps.

## 2. Experimental setup

- **Environment:** `CarRacing-v3`, `continuous=False`, frame skip of 4, and 4 stacked grayscale frames.
- **Method:** DQN with experience replay and hard target-network updates.
- **Change from baseline:** Initial baseline experiment.
- **Fixed settings:** Learning rate `1e-4`, batch size `64`, replay capacity `50,000`, and discount factor `0.99`.
- **Configuration file:** `configs/dqn_baseline.json`
- **Training seeds:** `0`, `1`, and `2`.
- **Evaluation:** 10 episodes using seeds `1000`–`1009`, with epsilon set to `0`.
- **Training budget:** 300,000 environment steps per run.

## 3. Success criteria

The mean evaluation reward must exceed 100 in at least two of the three training runs.

## 6. Conclusion

The result is inconclusive. Only one of the three runs exceeded the target evaluation reward of 100. Variation between runs is substantial, so the current evidence is insufficient to conclude that the agent reliably meets the success criterion.

Before comparing this baseline with Genetic DQN, investigate whether performance changes with a more suitable exploration schedule or a larger training budget. Change one main factor at a time and keep the evaluation procedure consistent.
