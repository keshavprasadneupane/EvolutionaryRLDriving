## Training Infrastructure: Interrupt-Based Replay Persistence

Added replay-buffer persistence to the `KeyboardInterrupt` handler in `train.py`.

- Regular checkpoints save without replay data, keeping them lightweight.
- On `Ctrl+C`, the handler saves the agent checkpoint with `include_replay=True`.
- This preserves accumulated experience for resuming interrupted training.
- Normal completion does not require a replay-buffer snapshot.

This avoids repeatedly writing several gigabytes of replay data while retaining recovery capability for unfinished runs.
